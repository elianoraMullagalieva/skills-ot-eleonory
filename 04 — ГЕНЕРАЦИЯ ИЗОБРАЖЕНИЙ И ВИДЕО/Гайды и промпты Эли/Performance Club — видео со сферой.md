Генерируем видео для Performance Club — разбитая стеклянная сфера/линза в космическом стиле.

**Why:** Брендовый визуальный контент для Performance Club с фирменными цветами.
**How to apply:** Использовать промпт ниже в Higgsfield Video, модель Nano Banana Pro или 2.

## Фирменные цвета Performance Club
- #D7F731 — кислотный лимонный (акцент, 5% присутствия)
- #02101C — почти чёрный тёмно-синий (фон)
- #71E0EA — ледяной голубой циан (основной свет)
- #F3F3F3 — белый (нейтральный)
- #181818 — графит

## Рабочий промпт (для 16:9 горизонтального видео)

```
Image 1-5 = exact style, atmosphere and lighting reference.

COMPOSITION — wide cinematic 16:9 horizontal frame:
The shattered glass sphere occupies only 40-50% of frame height,
centered or slightly left-of-center. Wide empty dark space on all sides.
The sphere must be FULLY visible with breathing room around it.
Do NOT crop or cut the sphere edges.

SCENE: Shattered crystal sphere / broken glass lens floating in deep space.
Fractures radiate outward from center like a starburst.
Light explodes from within through the cracks.

COLORS:
- Background: deep dark space #02101C, almost black
- Core light: icy cyan-blue #71E0EA, cold volumetric glow
- 2-3 crack edges: whisper-level acid lemon #D7F731 
  (barely visible, 5% presence only, NOT dominant)
- Glass shards: dark #181818 with translucent highlights

IMPORTANT FOR ANIMATION:
Leave significant negative space left and right of the sphere.
The sphere should have room to breathe — not filling the full frame.
This allows cinematic slow zoom without cropping.

Style: IMAX cinematic, ultra-detailed glass physics, 
deep space atmosphere, volumetric light diffusion, 
photorealistic CGI, dark luxury aesthetic.
```
